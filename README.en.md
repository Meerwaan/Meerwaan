## About me

I am a **full-stack developer** who owns the whole chain of a product: scoping, data model, API, interface, payments, deployment, and the follow-up once real users are on it.

Since 2025 I have been designing and shipping **several SaaS products solo**, including **Reputap**, in production, which collects Google reviews through NFC for multi-location businesses. Before that, I was the **sole developer** of a neuropsychological follow-up tool at **Neocortex**, from architecture to go-live in practitioners' offices. Two work-study positions, a Master's degree in computer engineering management at **ESIEE IT (Coding Factory)** and a business and entrepreneurship semester at **Université Laval (Québec)**: I can build a product, and I can frame it as a business.

**How I work**
- 📐 **A spec before the code.** Routes, models and edge cases are written down before the first line.
- 🧩 **Readable code.** Short files, one responsibility per unit, strict TypeScript, Zod validation at the boundaries.
- 🚀 **Ship, don't demo.** I would rather have a system running for real users than a perfect demo.
- 📊 **Measure with real data.** Every product decision is backed by what users actually do.

**What I am looking for**
A **full-time full-stack product engineer role** in a team that ships, or **short, well-scoped freelance missions**. On site in the Paris region, remote anywhere. I reply within 48 hours.
